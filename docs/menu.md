# Menu

`menu.tscn` → `MenuShell`, and `godot/scripts/menu/`. Split out of `CLAUDE.md`; read this before touching any menu screen. **Jackson owns this area** — see the ownership note in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Menu** (`menu.tscn` → `MenuShell`): the native Godot menu, and the only menu. It began as a port of an HTML build (`web-menu/`, since deleted — a browser page cannot go in the iOS app) and was a Brawl Stars lobby clone: profile and season plates top-left, currency pills top-right, two flanking columns of painted icon chips, mode plate and PLAY bottom-right, over a pre-rendered auditorium JPEG. **That was overhauled wholesale.** Copy and config still live in `godot/data/brawlers.json` and `godot/data/game.json` — **game.json holds only what a screen draws now** (`season`, `startingBrawlers`, `opponents`, `modes`, `shop`, `passRewards`, `trophyRoad`); `news`, `friends`, `club`, `inbox`, `upcoming`, `quests`, `leaderboard` and `gameLog` were deleted on 7 Sep, a third of the file, having been shipped in the bundle for screens that no longer exist; `include_filter="data/*.json"` in `export_presets.cfg` is what keeps them in the iOS build — without it the menu ships with no data.

- **The reference world is a printed game programme on a lit stage.** The type is the programme's (display **Anton**, labels and body **Barlow Condensed**, a **deliberate hole** between utility at 26–30 and display at 44+) and gold is the only colour with a job (earned / active / yours). The material is Jackson's 6 Sep mockup: **rounded cards with a one-pixel edge** (`MenuUI.RADIUS` = 12, chips at 6 — one radius, not "small"), no bevel, no shadow, no gradient, and one concession to depth: a darker bottom edge on pressables (`MenuUI._keyed`) so a button reads as a key. Coloured **stat bars** (`stat_bar_row`) and round **medallions** (`medallion`) are the two pictures the system draws itself.
- **Nothing in the menu's utility tier clears Apple's 11 pt floor** — measured, not feared. Stage px convert at **x0.364** on an iPhone 15 in landscape, so 11 pt is **30.2 stage px** and the tier's own ceiling is 30; all 39 sized utility labels were under it when counted on 7 Sep. Roster, Shop and Events hold the system's own 26 floor **in their own screen files**; Season and Home do not, and **Season cannot** without showing fewer Pass tiers at once, because its page is a fixed 816 px that does not scroll. **The "conformed" screens are not actually clear of sub-26 type, and the two that break it are shared helpers rather than screen code** — `MenuUI.block()`'s rule text is 22 (every Shop block head: "ONE PULL, ODDS PRINTED", "N COINS IN HAND", "RESETS IN…") and `MenuScreen.topbar`'s sub is 24 (Roster's "9 OF 9", on every pushed screen). Counting per screen file misses both, which is how the claim came out cleaner than the build. Corrected 7 Sep 2026 after grepping the sizes rather than the diff. This is `todo 1.3`, the last open P0, and it is a redesign of the deliberate hole in the scale rather than a multiply — do not bump sizes screen by screen.
- **There are no rules any more** (6 Sep, Jackson's layout pass — ROADMAP **D1**). The hairlines that underlined every section head and stat row and capped home top and bottom read as stray lines on a phone, not as structure; `MenuUI.section` and `stat_line` are label-only now and spacing separates blocks. `MenuUI.rule()` survives for the few places a line IS the content — the threshold underline on the Season rails, the flag down a mode card. Do not put a rule back "to separate" anything.
- **The utility tier moved from 17–22 to 26–30 stage px** for todo 1.3: on the phone the menu stage converts at x0.364 px→pt, so 19 px was 6.9 pt against Apple's 11 pt floor. It moved as a tier (`label` defaults to 26, `body` to 30, stat keys 26, units 22) and the display sizes it met moved with it (stat figures 44–46, ability names 36); the hole is still there, one step up.
- **Five zones, and the corners always mean the same thing.** Top-left is *who you are*: on home the name and record; on every pushed screen the back arrow in a 68 px rounded square (`MenuUI.square_button("back")`), including the room screen. Top-right is the currency readout — the pack's coin and gem beside a figure with its name under it, `MenuShell.currency_readout` — a hairline divider, then the menu as three bars over the word MENU (`MenuUI.menu_button`), never a MENU button. The left flank is number, role chip, name, title, then five **stat bars** — a glyph box (`svg/stat_*.svg`), the stat name over a bar filled against the roster's best (`HomeScreen._measure_roster`; reload's bar shows quickness, so the fastest reload fills it), the figure and its unit. The right flank is two **ability cards** — a medallion whose glyph is the ability's kits.gd Style (`svg/style_<style>.svg`, carried on `MenuData` as `attack.style` / `super.style`), the tier word in the accent colour (blue attack, green Super), the name, and the write-up's first sentence — then the **record card** (trophy from the pack, `rank` and `power` glyphs, rows separated by rules, which are content there). Bottom-left is the nav, bottom-right the mode plate (gold edge, `3V3 / NOBLES CUP ›`) and PLAY, 8 px apart on the same 108 px baseline, PLAY the biggest thing on the screen. Under the fighter's feet one hint; nothing else near him.
- **The nav bar belongs to the shell, not to home** (`MenuShell._build_nav`, `MenuUI.nav_tab`). ROSTER / SEASON / SHOP / WIFI are words — built like `link`, so each sizes to its word — on every screen, the current one white with a gold bar under it (`_active_tab` reads the topmost non-popup screen's `screen_name`; home lights none), hidden only under a popup. Tapping the lit tab does nothing; tapping another pops the stack and pushes the target. Screens leave it its strip: `MenuScreen.fill_content`/`scroll_content` keep `MenuUI.NAV_H` (150) clear at the bottom and the room screen is inset the same amount. A screen that lays out to a fixed height (Season's two rails) has to fit above it — that is why its rails and header got shorter.
- **The icon pack** (`assets/menu/profile/`, 256 px PNGs with clean alpha, mipmapped; `MenuUI.pack_icon` with an svg fallback) supplies the coin, gem and trophy; the stage painting is `assets/menu/background/stage.jpg`; the rest of the pictures are script-drawn svgs (pipeline 3). Nothing in the pack with a face in it is used.
- **Five surfaces, down from thirteen.** Home, Roster, Season, Shop, Wifi, plus the settings/profile popups. Deleted outright: News, Club, Inbox, Matchmaking-as-a-screen, the roster grid's detail card, and the storefront half of Shop. They were furniture for a live-service game this is not.
- **Home is also the detail screen** (`home_screen.gd`). The left flank is the selected fighter's identity and stat column, the right is their attack/Super write-ups and record; the middle is the live fighter. **This is what stops the width being dead space** — a single figure centred on a 1920 stage leaves two empty thirds, which is exactly the gap Brawl Stars fills with icon columns, and filling them with the fighter's own data means the width carries content instead of navigation. It gets better as the stage widens on a phone. Because home shows the detail, `RosterScreen` is a plain picker that selects and returns (two taps to change fighter, down from five).
- **Stats print as figures, not tiers.** `MenuData._merge` emits both: `speed`/`range`/`reload` are the tiered labels ("Very Fast"), and `speed_value`/`range_value`/`reload_value` are the raw numbers the flank column prints. A word in a table of numerals breaks the column and says less. Range is in **tiles**, the unit the balance work is done in.
- **The stage is a painting, lit per fighter.** `MenuShell.STAGE_BACKDROP` is the icon pack's dark hall with a lit floor, scaled to cover the stage; `MenuStage` renders the fighter over it on a **transparent** viewport (no ground plane, no fog — the arena-floor set it used to draw is gone, and the fog constants with it are inert) with a 2D pool and two rings under his feet (`_build_floor_ring`, centred on `HomeScreen.FEET_Y`). **The light takes the kit colour**: `STAGE_TINT_SHADER` recolours the painting by its own luminance — the hall stays navy where it is dark, the floor becomes the kit colour where it is lit — and the rings and pool follow (`_light_stage`, called from `select_brawler`), so nine fighters get nine stages from one file. A kit may name its own painting with a `stage` key and gets it untinted. The fighter is framed to `MenuStage.FILL` (0.64) of the stage height about a fixed look point, which is why his feet land at a known `FEET_Y` with no per-image floor line — the framing chain the old JPEG needed (`FLOOR_FRAC`, `BRAWLER_VIEW`, `_place_brawler`) is still gone.
- **Season and Shop are built out of one block** (`MenuUI.block(icon, title, rule)` → `[card, body]`): a bordered card with a glyph, a name and the rule that governs what is inside it. Two dense screens sharing it is what makes them read as one system rather than two layouts with the same palette. Two more shared pieces exist for the same reason: **`MenuUI.reward_glyph(kind)`** and **`MenuUI.reward_name(kind, amount)`** — a Dawg Treat that is a bone on one screen and a word on the other is two systems, and Shop had been calling the same thing "50 POWER POINTS" where Season called it "50 POWER PTS". Naming that needs a fighter looked up (`brawler`, `skin`, `pin`) stays with the caller, which is why `reward_name` returns `""` for those.
- **Shop opens on the Dawg Treat** (`shop_screen.gd`, 7 Sep). It was three lists — nine near-identical POWER UP rows with an UPGRADE button each, filling the screen, and the Treat below the fold under them. The Treat is the only thing on the page with an occasion to it, so it is the first block; the fighters are cards with their own faces on them; the odds are a row of seven rarity chips rather than a seven-line table, which is a third of the height and puts the colours in a scale. **The power card is deliberately NOT the button** — everywhere else in the menu a whole tile is pressable, and here it must not be, because claiming a Trophy Road reward is free and this spends 200 coins a tap. A cost earns an explicit control.
- **Events draws the icon each mode already names** (`modes_screen.gd`, 7 Sep). Every mode carries an `icon` in game.json — `bulldog`, `gem`, `brawl_ball`, `coin`, `power_point`, `star_drop` — and nothing drew them; the Cup's ball (`svg/ball.svg`) was drawn to complete the set, and `bulldog` comes from the profile pack rather than the svg folder. Two real bugs went with the relayout: **the SELECT button was built `"navy"`, which `MenuUI.fill_for` resolves to `PANEL`** — the same fill as the card under it — so the only mode you could not already play offered a word floating in the dark; and each card still carried a hairline rule as a separator, which the 6 Sep pass removed everywhere else. The five unbuilt modes are small dim cards under a PLANNED head rather than rows, so the screen reads as a plan rather than as a list of things that do not work.

- **The design system carries no dead helpers.** `plate_colors` (todo 5.1's "either it earns its place or it goes" — it had been flattened to three copies of one fill in the overhaul and had no caller since), `stat_row`/`stat_line`, `body_font_700`, `disabled_button`, `art_button` and `chip` were all removed on 7 Sep with zero callers between them. Check with `grep -rn "\.name(" godot --include="*.gd"` before adding one back; git history has them.

- **The roster is a wall of faces, not a table** (`roster_screen.gd`, 7 Sep). It was a numbered table until then, and the argument for it — nine rows fit one screen and put the figures in columns you can compare down — was true and beside the point. **You do not pick a fighter by comparing trophy counts**; you pick the one you want to play and you recognise them by face. One tile each: the portrait render on a ground of the kit's colour, the roster number top-left, a padlock or SELECTED top-right, then name, role and the two figures small in a footer. `_columns()` is `ceil(n / 2)`, so it is two rows at any roster size — 5+4 today, 6+6 at the twelve the beta bar asks for — and past twelve it needs a scroll or a third row. Nova has no render (`todo 4.1`) and gets her initial in the display face; a locked fighter is dimmed rather than hidden, because which fighter it is *is* the reason to go and unlock them. **The ground is a `Panel`, not a `PanelContainer`** — a container lays its children out to fill it and ignores their anchors, which put the number and the SELECTED mark on top of each other.

- **`MenuUI.hex()` takes a Color as well as a string.** game.json carries colours as `"#57c81e"`; `kits.gd` carries them as real `Color`s that `MenuData` passes straight through. Round-tripping one through `str()` yields `"(0.2, 0.88, 0.78, 1)"`, which is not a colour name — Godot logged an error per fighter and returned black, which is why the roster's team blocks came out unpainted.
- **Layout is in "stage pixels".** Screens are authored against a 1920×1080 stage; `MenuShell._fit_stage` scales it to the device and grows it to COVER the display — *wider* than 16:9 (a phone) it gains width (2017 px of it on an iPhone 15), *narrower* (a 16:10 MacBook in fullscreen) it gains height. Keeping the height at 1080 whatever the display was tried first: on the 16:10 screen the picture became a letterboxed band while the chrome, anchored to the display, sat in the bars above and below it, which read as a crop. Anything that must sit under the fighter's feet hangs off `HomeScreen.FEET_FRAC` (a proportion of the stage height), not a pixel row, because `MenuStage` frames him to the stage height. The iOS safe area is honoured by insetting the `chrome` node, **not** by shrinking the stage: see `docs/phone_fit.md`, where doing it the other way is written up as one of the two P0s.
- **Files.** `scripts/menu/`: `menu_data.gd` (JSON + `Kits` merge — copy from the JSON, live balance from `kits.gd`; a kit with no JSON entry is synthesised, though as of Nova's entry there is no longer one. The JSON's own `stats` block is read by nothing; the `loadout` dict `_merge` emits has no reader either, since the screen that drew it was the roster detail card the overhaul deleted), `menu_ui.gd` (the design system), `menu_screen.gd`/`menu_popup.gd` (screen + popup bases), `menu_audio.gd` (every SFX synthesized at runtime — no SFX files ship), `home_screen.gd`, `roster_screen.gd`, `season_screen.gd`, `shop_screen.gd`, `modes_screen.gd`, `menu_popups.gd` (settings + profile). Every route in is `MenuShell.show_screen(name)`, which keeps the old thirteen-screen names as aliases so a stale hook lands somewhere sensible.
- **A screen leaving `_stack` is NOT a screen that has left the glass**, and every piece of shell state that keys off the stack has to allow for the 0.24 s (`MenuShell.SCREEN_EXIT`) its exit animation still runs for. Both halves of the "the roster's names overlap" bug were this one mistake. `push_screen`'s guard against "two screens printed on top of each other" hides `_stack[-1]`, which a screen mid-exit is no longer — and the nav bar pops and pushes in a single press, so the outgoing screen was invisible to exactly the guard written for it; `_drop_closing()` now sweeps `screens_root` for any `MenuScreen` not in the stack and drops it (hiding as well as freeing, since `queue_free` lands at the end of the frame). And `_update_stage_dim` restored `home` and the 3D fighter at the TOP of the exit, so a screen dissolved across them — two titles, two currency readouts in the same corner, nine names over the flanks. Home comes back when the screen has actually gone, which makes the exit fade over the painted stage, matching what the entrance always did. It then needs its own fade (`HOME_FADE`) because it is no longer being uncovered, and `brawler_view` must fade with it — it is home's SIBLING, so home's `modulate` does not reach it.
- **RAISE A POPUP LAST. `push_screen` hides `_stack[-1]`, and a popup is on that stack.** So "open the reward, then rebuild the screen under it" hides the reward on the frame it opened, and a popup whose reward is only granted when you tap it then grants nothing at all — `shop_screen.gd` opened a Dawg Treat and called `_reopen()` after it, which took 1,000 coins and gave back a plain Shop page for the whole life of the feature. It survived because the popup is still *in* the stack, so pressing BACK afterwards un-hides it, and because nobody pressed the button — the screen was judged as a picture. `season_screen.gd:_claim` has always had the order right (`_reopen()`, then the popup); make every new call site match it, and remember the check is a press, not a screenshot. Fixed and verified 7 Sep 2026 by probing the live path for `popup=true visible=?`.
- **Godot layout gotchas this menu hit.** `PanelContainer` stretches *every* child to fill it, so a card with overlays must be `MenuUI.card()` — a plain `Panel` — with `MenuUI.card_body()`. `set_anchors_and_offsets_preset(..., PRESET_MODE_MINSIZE)` reads a minimum size a node does not have until it is in the tree; use `MenuUI.pin()`. A `ScrollContainer` has no minimum height, so a popup built around one collapses to its title bar.
- **Unlocks are real.** `SaveGame.ensure_loaded` seeds only `MenuData.starting_brawlers()` (Nova); everyone else is locked, shows their `unlockHint` in place of their numbers on their roster tile, and arrives through a named Trophy Road milestone or a Dawg Treat. **Leon, Anders, Hammy and Ayaan have no named unlock**: the road covers Sanjit, Tony, Kovacs and Henry, so those four are reachable only through a Treat. Settings → Developer mode unlocks everyone on the current save.
- **Season merges Trophy Road and the Pass** (`season_screen.gd`), because they answer the same question and differ only in whether they reset. Road entries are `{trophies, reward: {kind, amount|id}}` (the older flat `{trophies, kind, ...}` is still accepted by `_payload`); Pass claims are keyed `pass:<tier>:<free|premium>`. Kinds: `coins`, `gems`, `power_points`, `bling`, `dawg_treat`, `star_drop`, `skin`, `pin`, `brawler` (named, by `id`) and `brawler_drop` (random, rarity-weighted, no duplicates). Adding a kind means touching `_reward_name`, `_reward_icon`, `_claim` and `SaveGame.grant` together. Laid out to Jackson's 7 Sep mockup, and three things about it are load-bearing:
  - **A reward is a picture of itself.** The rails used to set every reward as words — "250 COINS" in the display face — on the argument that a word reads faster down a rail and ships no file. Twenty columns of words is a wall of text, and what is being scanned for is *which kind* of reward, which is what a glyph answers first. Glyphs come from `assets/menu/svg/` (`dawg_treat`, `crown`, `ticket` and `pin` were drawn for this); a fighter, a skin or a pin shows the roster's own portrait render in a tile of the kit's colour, never a second illustration of them.
  - **A claimable card IS the button** (`_reward_card` returns a `Button` in that state, a `PanelContainer` otherwise). A gold CLAIM button *inside* the card was the obvious build and does not fit: `MenuUI.button` carries 16px of content margin and the display face's line box is 1.64x its size, so a 20px CLAIM cannot be shorter than 65 — half the tile — and every claimable column grew taller than its neighbours and pushed the rail out of its block. `MenuUI.button` now takes a `pad`, but the fix here was to stop nesting a button at all: the tile is also the bigger tap target on a phone. The state line is a word in all three states — tick, padlock, or CLAIM.
  - **The heights add up to 816 stage pixels and are checked, not estimated.** Nothing on this screen scrolls vertically, so `HEADER_H + ROAD_H + PASS_H + 2 gaps` has to fit between the top bar and `MenuUI.NAV_H`; overshoot puts the Pass's premium lane under the nav. Anton's line box is ~1.64x its font size and Barlow's ~1.2x, which is why every hand-computed budget here came out short — print `get_global_rect()` for the blocks and the nav rather than adding up font sizes. On a stage taller than 16:9 the blocks and the cards take the slack (`_grow`), because parked at the bottom it reads as a band of nothing; a `ScrollContainer` only stretches its child on the disabled axis if the CHILD carries `SIZE_EXPAND`.
- **Shop is a sink, not a store.** The gem packs priced in dollars, the bundles and the row of tiles reading PURCHASED are gone; what is left is the loop that was already real — `award_match` grants coins, so power levels, Dawg Treats and the resource swaps are where they go. `_affordable_deals` filters `game.json` by currency rather than hardcoding a list, so a dollar-priced item added to the JSON simply will not appear. The Treat's odds are printed from the same `TREAT_TIERS` that `_roll_tier` runs on.
- **Wifi play** is the WIFI nav tab, which opens `room_screen.gd` inside a bare `MenuScreen` host. The room is still `ui_kit.gd`-styled inside — the one corner the overhaul did not touch — but its back button is the menu's square arrow, so the corner matches.
- **`godot/assets/menu/` went from 124 files to 34**, and then gained `profile/` (the nine pack icons) and `svg/menu.svg`. What remains is what is still referenced: three fonts, `portraits/` (used by the versus screen, the results card and the loading screen — *not* by the menu), `svg/`, `profile/`, `audio/` (two MP3s: lobby music and the battle track — the versus sting was a Brawl Stars rip and is deleted, so the versus screen is silent until a synthesized sting replaces it) and `background/loading_keyart.jpg`. `bg.png`, the `btn_*` chips, `icons/`, `cards/`, `treats/`, `decor/` and `pack/` were all deleted with the screens that used them.
