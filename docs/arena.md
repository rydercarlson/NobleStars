# Arena

Bushes, terrain and the gas ring. Split out of `CLAUDE.md`; read this before touching `arena.gd` or `gas_ring.gd`.

## Bushes

**Bushes** (`arena.gd`, the block under `GRASS_MODEL`): each `b` tile gets two MultiMesh instances — a flat **skirt** quad sized exactly to `Kits.TILE` and a `tall_grass.glb` **canopy** on top. The skirt is what makes a bush read as a *tile*: adjacent quads meet with no seam, so a patch becomes one contiguous dark shape, and its outline is merge-aware (`_open_edges` packs "this side has no bush neighbour" into `INSTANCE_CUSTOM`; the shader rims only those sides, so a 3x3 patch is one shape and not nine squares). The canopy has no yaw/scale jitter on purpose — the old jitter existed to stop a field reading as tiled, which is now exactly the goal. Both layers fade any instance within `Kits.BUSH_REVEAL` of `reveal_center`, which `main.gd:_update_concealment` points at the player every physics frame, so the concealment radius `can_see()` enforces is the same one you can see through. Two gotchas: both layers are transparent and never get a depth sort, which works only because `_build` appends bushes row-major from the map's -Z edge (the far side of a camera at +Z) — that buffer order *is* the painter's order; and the skirt carries `render_priority = -1` so it never flips in front of its own canopy.

## Terrain

**Terrain** (`arena.gd`, the "terrain look" block) — the floor, the walls and the water are three fragment shaders over flat colour, no art files, for the same reason the sounds are synthesized: a fixed steep camera only ever shows one angle.

- **Shadows were switched on and drew nothing for the life of the project.** The match camera sits 105.5 m back behind a 7° lens, so the whole arena is further away than a directional light's **100 m default `directional_shadow_max_distance`** — correct shadows, entirely outside the volume they get drawn in. `Arena.make_sun()` sets 145 m with one orthogonal split. Anything that renders the arena has to use `Arena.make_sun()` / `make_environment()`; a second copy of those numbers is how the tool and the game drift apart.
- **Walls and water share the bushes' merge-aware outline.** Each tile's "which of my four sides has no neighbour" mask rides an **`instance uniform`** (`open_edges`), so all ~400 walls share one material and one shader and still each get their own rim — and `open_at` hands the four neighbours of a hole a fresh mask, so a wall shot out re-outlines what is left. `_open_sides` treats off-map as matching, so the border ring is not outlined against the void.
- **Water is opaque now.** The old translucent slabs blended against the floor *and* against each other, so a pond showed a grid of seams where its tiles overlapped. Foam width is the constant to watch: a single-tile pond is only 2 m across, and the first pass at 24% of a tile per side left almost no water in the middle of one.
- **A tile-scale checker cannot be the floor's variation.** However faint it is made it reads as a chessboard; two octaves of value noise at 18 m and 6 m read as ground. The checker is still there at ~2%, under the noise.
- **The arena sits on a slab inside a surround plane.** Past the map edge used to be raw sky. `SURROUND_HORIZON` is also `Environment.background_color`, so the scenery meets the background with no seam — change one and change the other.
- **Nobles Cup's pitch markings are painted by the floor shader** (`pitch_mark`, from `_playable_rect()`), not laid down as geometry, so a line costs nothing and cannot z-fight the grass. Showdown passes `pitch_lines = 0`.
- **Wall height is purely visual** — `Lob` is a `Node3D` with no collision and arcs over walls logically, `begin_leap` sweeps terrain itself, and both the LOS ray (y = 1) and every projectile (y = 1) already sit inside a 1.5 m box. `Arena.WALL_HEIGHT` can be retuned on looks alone.

## The gas ring

**The gas ring** (`gas_ring.gd`) — Showdown's shrinking safe zone, and the same shaders-over-art-files rule as the terrain.

- **`inset` is a FLOAT and everything reads the eased value, the rules included.** It steps on exactly the old cadence (`FIRST_SHRINK_DELAY` 18 s, then `TILES_PER_SHRINK` 3 every `SHRINK_INTERVAL` 12 s) but takes `SHRINK_EASE` = 3 s to travel each step instead of teleporting. Easing only the *visuals* was the alternative and it is a lie the player cannot see through — for the whole ease you would burn on ground that reads as safe. Everything outside the file already went through `contains()` / `depth_inside()` / `safe_min()` / `safe_max()` / `safe_center()`, all floats, so bots' `gas_depth` steering got a continuously moving edge for nothing.
- **The rate is what to check when retuning, not the duration.** A smoothstep peaks at 1.5x its average: 3.9 m in 3 s tops out at 1.95 m/s against `Kits.SPEED_VERY_SLOW`. The wall must stay walkable-out-of. **`TILES_PER_SHRINK` is in TILES, so it had to go 2 → 3 when the tile shrank** — at 2 the ring needed 15 steps instead of 10 on the 61-tile map and full closure went from 126 s to 186 s, which also lengthens every `NS3_SIM` match by half.
- **The cloud bank is parametric, not reseeded.** Every instance stores its edge, its position along it and its own jitter/scale/yaw, generated once at a constant seed; world transforms are recomputed from the eased edge every frame. The old code did `rng.seed = 7 + inset` and rebuilt, so the whole pattern popped on every step. The count per edge is fixed and the clouds *bunch* as the ring closes rather than being culled — culling puts the pop back, and dropping instances from the buffer breaks the only thing that sorts a MultiMesh of transparent instances, which is the order they were written in (far edge, flanks, near edge = back-to-front at +Z). The MultiMesh needs `PHYSICS_INTERPOLATION_MODE_OFF` because it is driven from `_process`.
- **A translucent fill loses to grass, and that is a LINEAR-space fact.** The old four `BoxMesh`es at 22% read as a purple filter over the picture, not as gas: blending happens in linear, where grass's green channel is 0.45 against a dark violet's 0.003, so any plausible-looking violet composites to a dead grey-green. The fill is one shader over two quads now (a mat at ankle height, a haze at 2.6 m drifting faster, so the pair separates under the camera pitch), and it is **nearly opaque with almost no green** — at 0.93 alpha `ALBEDO` is more or less the answer. It can afford that because the mat is under a fighter's feet and never occludes anyone; only the thin haze is ever between the camera and a body.
- The front is the safe rectangle's box SDF pushed about by a noise octave — that is what stops it reading as a rectangle. Both quads carry a `rim_fade` so they end before their own straight edge does (the haze is 2.6 m up, so its rectangle projects clear of the arena slab's lip and was the most obviously wrong line on screen).
- **`gas_cloud.glb` is preloaded in `Loading.to_match`** (Showdown only), because the bank is now built on the match's first frame rather than at the first shrink — the same trap as `power_cube.glb`.


## Power-cube performance

**Power-cube performance:** `main.gd` must keep `power_cube.glb` preloaded. Loading the 21 MB source scene inside `_spawn_cube` puts disk/texture work on the loot box's fatal-hit frame and causes a visible freeze. The extracted 4096×4096 base-color and metallic/roughness textures are intentionally capped at 512 via their `.png.import` files (about 582 KB imported total instead of 16.6 MB); preserve that cap and reimport after changing either texture.
